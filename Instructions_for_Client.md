# 🗺️ Math-Laro: Bislig Edition – Explorer’s Guide

Welcome to **Bislig**, Guardian! This guide will help you navigate the math-filled regions of our province and master the ancient **Decoding Shield** to solve word problems and protect our home.

---

## 🎒 1. Getting Started

### Creating Your Profile
1. On the Title Screen, enter your **Explorer Name** in the box.
2. Click **START ADVENTURE** to begin your journey.
3. The game will automatically remember your progress! The next time you play, you can choose **LOAD GAME** to pick up right where you left off.

> [!TIP]
> Each student can have their own profile. You can see how many quests you've cleared and how many Math-Coins you've earned in the Load Menu.

---

## 🗺️ 2. The Quest Map

The map of Bislig is divided into four legendary regions, each focusing on a different math power:

1.  **Addition Meadows**: The starting fields for young guardians.
2.  **Subtraction Springs**: Cooling waters for logic training.
3.  **Multiplication Mountains**: Steep peaks for master calculators.
4.  **Division Delta**: The final challenge in the winding rivers.

### How to Start a Quest
- Look for **Golden Quest Icons** on the map.
- Click a quest to see its title and the reward you will earn.
- Click the **PLAY** button to enter the quest and start solving!

---

## 🛡️ 3. Solving Quests: The Decoding Shield

Every math quest in Bislig uses the **Decoding Shield**. Instead of just guessing the answer, you must use logic to "decode" the word problem in 4 steps:

### Step 1: What is Asked?
Read the story carefully. What is the goal? Choose the correct question from the options provided.

### Step 2: What are Given?
What numbers and facts do we already know from the story? Select the correct pieces of information.

### Step 3: Which Operation?
Should you use **Addition (+)**, **Subtraction (-)**, **Multiplication (×)**, or **Division (÷)**? Pick the right operation to unlock the next step.

### Step 4: Number Sentence
Combine your facts and operation into a complete equation (e.g., `45 + 38 = n`).

### 🌟 Final Answer
Once the shield is complete, use your keyboard or the on-screen number pad to type the final result. Click **SUBMIT** to clear the quest!

---

## 💎 4. Rewards & Power-Cards

As a Math Guardian, you earn rewards for your hard work:

*   **Math-Coins**: You earn these for every step of the Decoding Shield you get right. Use them to show off your rank!
*   **Power-Cards (Hints)**: If you get stuck, click on the **Lightbulb (Power-Card)** icon. It will highlight the correct answer for you! You earn new Power-Cards every time you successfully clear a full quest.

---

## ⚙️ 5. Game Controls & Menu

### The HUD (Heads-Up Display)
- **Coins & Cards**: Your current stats are always shown at the top.
- **MENU Button**: Access the game menu anytime.
- **🔊/🔇 Mute Button**: Toggle the adventure background music on or off.

### The Game Menu
- **SAVE GAME**: Manually save your progress at any time.
- **LOAD LAST SAVE**: If you want to restart your current quest, use this to jump back to your last save.
- **QUIT TO HOME**: Safely exit back to the title screen.

---

## 🎵 6. Customizing Your Game (For Teachers/Clients)

You can easily change the music and sound effects:
- **Background Music**: Place an MP3 file named `adventure_bgm.mp3` in the `/public/music/` folder.
- **Click Sound**: Place a WAV file named `click.wav` in the `/public/music/` folder.
